Three people, one intake, before the consult

Patient
Speaks or types in Thai, Italian or English. A voice agent reads the question aloud; the patient checks and corrects the transcript before it is sent.
Nurse
Reads an English summary (design goal: quick to scan). Clicks a field to see the patient's own sentence. Approves, edits or takes over.
Pharmacist
Reconciles the medicines the patient named, brand to generic, with alerts on duplicate paracetamol, allergy conflicts and interactions.
Clinic manager
Sees the queue by urgency, escalations, abstain rate and model latency.

Safety by design, in five answers

Wrong or unsupported answers
No verified quote means "needs check". Output is schema-checked; quotes must match the patient's words. Injection handling is not yet evaluated end to end.
Sensitive information
Redaction before storage or any model call. Synthetic data only. Keys stay server-side.
Uncertain results
Confidence is shown on every field. Low confidence or provider failure: abstain, "needs human review".
High-risk decisions
Rules run before any model. The Gemma screen is add-only, shown as "model-suggested, confirm".
Human review
Nothing is final until a clinician approves. Every action is in the append-only audit log.
Thai patient, chest pain and sweating in the first message: the nurse screen is interrupted, the patient's sentence is highlighted, the nurse acknowledges or marks a false alarm with a reason. The patient phone shows a calm "go to the desk now" screen.
Mechanisms and code paths: pitch/SAFETY.md. Cross-vendor review: Claude read-only reviewed the Codex engine (docs/reports/w6-engine-review.md) and QA scripts hit every route (docs/reports/w7-qa-bugs.md); findings fixed with regression tests; remaining limitations are listed in the submission.
