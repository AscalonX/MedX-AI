# How MedX handles the five Track 2 questions

Research prototype. Synthetic data only. It supports clinicians; it does not diagnose, treat, prescribe or decide urgency.

| Track 2 question | Mechanism | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|
| **Incorrect or unsupported responses** | Every extracted field must carry a quote copied from the patient's text. Code (not the model) checks the quote exists; if not, the field is `unsupported`, shown in pencil style, never as fact. Output is schema-validated; patient text is treated as data and quotes must match the patient's words; injection handling is not yet evaluated end to end. Measured as unsupported-claim rate with vs without verifier. | `apps/web/lib/pipeline/verify.ts`, `schema.ts`, `prompts.ts`; design in `docs/ai/DESIGN.md` §4, §6; metrics `docs/ai/METRICS.md` M1 to M3, M5 Consult "consider" items come only from a fixed, curated rule list (12 presentations), are doctor-only, are labelled "AI suggestion for clinician judgement, not a diagnosis", and are dropped from the signed note unless the doctor keeps them. Code: `apps/web/lib/pipeline/consult.ts`, `consultrules.json`. |
| **Sensitive information** | Raw patient text is redacted (names, phone, ID to tokens) before storage or any model call. Synthetic data only. API keys come from environment variables, server-side only. Audit log stores inputs by reference. | `apps/web/lib/pipeline/redact.ts`, `config.ts`, `store.ts`; rules in `CLAUDE.md` invariants 5 and 6 Consult listening uses the browser's cloud speech service and should start only after the patient agrees (consent step per safety review MF-10; confirm it is built before claiming it). |
| **Uncertain results** | Per-field confidence shown as ink density. Provider error, schema error, unsupported language, or a required field below the threshold gives `abstained` and "needs human review" with a reason, never a guess. Missing is not negative: "no allergies" must be a patient quote. Fault-injection tests require 100% abstain with zero asserted fields. | `apps/web/lib/pipeline/pipeline.ts`, `verify.ts`; `docs/clinical/SPEC.md` §5; `docs/ai/DESIGN.md` §5; `docs/ai/METRICS.md` M5 |
| **High-risk decisions** | About 18 deterministic red-flag rules (guideline-sourced) run first on every patient turn, before any model call; no model output can clear or downgrade a hit. A hit stops the intake, shows the patient a calm go-to-the-desk screen with no diagnosis, and interrupts the clinician screen with the highlighted sentence. A Gemma safety screen runs in parallel and is add-only: its hits need a verified quote and appear as "Model-suggested, confirm". If the screen fails, the rules still stand. Blind-holdout result: rules alone 7/10 emergencies caught (3/8 false alarms), which is why the screen exists. | `apps/web/lib/pipeline/redflags.ts`, `packages/shared/src/redflags.json`; `docs/clinical/SPEC.md` §3, §7; `docs/ai/DESIGN.md` §8; `eval/results/holdout-rules-only.md` Vitals give a NEWS2-based triage level that is only a suggestion: the nurse confirms it (`vitals.ts`, `triage.ts`). The AI never proposes drugs or doses. Orders and prescriptions are entered by the doctor; deterministic checks (allergy, interaction, duplicate, pregnancy, dose range) require a written override reason for high alerts, re-checked at submit and at pharmacist verify (`orders.ts`); the pharmacist verifies or returns each order. |
| **Human review** | Nothing is final until a clinician approves. Per field: approve, edit (single strike plus new value plus initials), or take over. A red flag can be acknowledged or marked false alarm with a reason, and stays visible. Every action (model and contract version, latency, endpoint, inputs by reference, outputs, overrides, reviewer) goes to an append-only audit log. | `apps/web/app/api/sessions/[id]/review/route.ts`, `takeover/route.ts`; `apps/web/lib/pipeline/store.ts`; `docs/clinical/SPEC.md` §4 |

## What we do not claim
- Not clinically validated; not a medical device; synthetic cases only, small n.
- The verifier proves a quote exists, not that its meaning is right.
- The model screen will miss some cases and raise some false alarms; both are reported.
- Voice input uses the phone's Apple or Google speech services, so audio leaves the app to that vendor.
- The demo has no clinician login: the reviewer id is self-declared.
- Redaction is pattern-based and imperfect.
- Clinic-side consult listening also goes to the browser's cloud speech service.
- The AI never diagnoses, orders, prescribes or confirms triage; clinicians do these in MedX. Triage is an adult NEWS2 suggestion (not pregnancy). Prescribing checks use a small rule set (59 drugs, 18 interactions, adult dose ceilings for 31) and miss what it lacks.
- Consider items come from a fixed 12-presentation list, not a complete differential.
- The Gemma on AMD Token Factory integration is pending a key; latency and accuracy numbers are [TBD-METRIC] until the run.
