## 3. Five cheap "wow" upgrades for the Presentation score (≤30 min each, banking-app spirit)

1. **Live receipt on the phone.** Fix 27 plus a teal check that draws in (240 ms, `pen` easing) when "In revisione" and then "Letto" tick. Film it split-screen: the nurse clicks approve and, a second later, the phone ticks "Letto". This is the "transfer complete" feeling of a banking app, and it shows the round trip judges want to see.
2. **Approval receipt card.** When the status reaches `approved`, the B-0xx gradient hero turns into a receipt: "Approved · Nurse A. Rossi · 15:28 · 7 fields, 4 from patient's words, 3 edited" with a check icon, plus a "Next case" pill. It reuses `GradientHero`, `Pill` and the counts already on the page, and gives the video a clear climax at 2:10.
3. **Ops "needs you now" balance card.** Hero = big red "2" "escalations not acknowledged" plus an "Open oldest" white pill. Below it, four tiles count up from 0 on first load (instant under reduced motion). This is the banking "available balance" pattern and passes the 5-second test.
4. **Emergency arrival moment in /review.** When the poll finds a new escalated session, the row slides in at the top with a one-time 420 ms red ring, and the tab title becomes "(1) Emergency · MedX Bridge". In the video, the nurse's screen visibly reacts the moment the Thai patient presses send.
5. **Scan to try.** A real QR code on the landing card, pointing to `/patient/` (static SVG, no dependency needed if one is already generated at build). It replaces the dev placeholder (fix 7) and lets judges try the intake on their own phone. Pair it with one line: "Try it: Italian, Thai or English".

Runner-up: make the evidence highlight more legible on camera. Use a 17px transcript, and when a field is selected show a small ocean label above the marked span ("Onset · since yesterday"). That ties the English value to the patient's words in one glance.

## Assumptions and limits

- J3 used the seeded B-009 and B-011. The brief named B-016 to B-018, but in this server those are live intakes created during the audit, not medication cases.
- Model-suggested flags (script 1:55) need the live AMD provider and were not tested on mock. Voice input was not tested because another agent owns it.
- I did not run a screen reader; the semantics were checked through the accessibility tree and DOM scripts.
